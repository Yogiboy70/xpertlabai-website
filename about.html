Domain expertise meets state-of-the-art AI, built from the ground up.

Xpert Lab AI was founded by Yogesh Saigal, Founder & CEO, who spent 25 years inside Indian and global organisations — at ABC Consultants, Randstad, Korn Ferry and Transearch — helping leadership teams hire, organise and grow. Those years showed him the same pattern everywhere: capable teams held back by Excel, manual processes and legacy software that was never designed for how Indian businesses actually work.

The first two years were spent in stealth — running real pilots and POCs with organisations across manufacturing, energy and industrial services, consulting and professional services, in India and the Middle East. We learned what actually works versus what looks good in a demo. The result is software tested against real data, real users and real resistance before a single rupee of marketing was spent.

What makes Xpert Lab AI different is not just the domain knowledge. It's the access to top AI and software professionals across geographies who ensure every product reflects the current state of the art. These are not wrappers around existing tools. Every core problem has been ideated from scratch and solved from the ground up.

What We Believe

Three beliefs. Every product.
No exceptions.

Most software companies built their products years ago and are now adding AI on top. We started from the other end: AI first, with the tracking, the records and the workflows built on top of it. That is what we mean by AI-native — and it shapes three beliefs.

💡

Software should earn its place.

We don't build features for the sake of a pricing tier. Every function in every product exists because a real practitioner in a real firm needed it. If it doesn't solve a problem, it doesn't ship.

🤖

AI should be invisible.

The best AI integration is the one your team uses without thinking about it, because it's woven into the workflow, not bolted on as a separate module with its own login and learning curve.

🔒

You should own your data.

Your client relationships, candidate database, pipeline, financials, these are your most valuable commercial assets. You should own them. Fully. Always. That's why every client runs on its own isolated environment — or inside your own cloud.
