Hiring & Talent Acquisition

Your hiring team runs on Excel.
Ours runs on AI.

Xpert ATS is an AI-native applicant tracking system for in-house hiring teams in MSMEs and large enterprises. It reads every CV, ranks every candidate against the role, keeps hiring managers in the loop and remembers every decision — so your recruiters spend their time on people, not spreadsheets.

Visit xpert-ats.com →Request a Demo
The Problem

Your recruiters are spending their week on admin, not on candidates.

Most hiring teams still track requisitions in a shared Excel, chase hiring managers on WhatsApp and screen hundreds of CVs by eye. Every role starts from zero. Feedback lives in someone's inbox. Nobody can say, with confidence, where a hire stands today.

Legacy ATS tools were built to store records, with AI bolted on later. Xpert ATS was built the other way round: AI first, with the tracking system built on top of it.

The result: your team gets its week back for the conversations that actually fill roles.

How It Works

From requisition to offer
in one AI-native flow.

1

Load

Open a requisition, drop in CVs from email, job boards or your old database. Every CV is read, structured and scored against the role — automatically.

2

Decide

Search in plain English, compare ranked candidates, send a one-click review link to the hiring manager and capture their verdict and comments.

3

Learn

Every shortlist, rejection and offer feeds Hiring Memory — so the system gets sharper about what your organisation actually hires for.

What It Does

Everything a modern hiring team needs.
Built AI-first.

🧠

AI Screening & Classification

Every CV read, scored and placed against the role with clear reasoning — far beyond keyword matching.

🔎

Plain-English AI Search

“Instrumentation engineer, 8+ years, oil & gas, ready to relocate.” Ranked results in seconds — including “find me people similar to this candidate”.

🤖

Shreyas, Your AI Agent

An agentic assistant inside every page. Ask “who is waiting on a hiring-manager decision?” and get a straight answer from live data.

✅

Hiring-Manager Review Links

Hiring managers review shortlists from a secure link — no login, no training. Their verdict and comments land straight in the candidate record.

🏅

Hiring Memory

Offers, runner-ups and post-interview shortlists are remembered and surfaced next time a similar role opens. Your talent pool compounds.

🤝

Agency & Vendor Portal

Give recruitment partners their own submission portal, track duplicates, and see fill rate, turnaround and quality on one scorecard.

📈

Hiring Analytics

Time-to-fill, stage conversion, source quality and offer-to-join — live, by location, function and hiring manager.

🌍

Multi-Location, Multi-Language

Plants, branches and countries in one pipeline. Built for India and deployed in the Middle East, with multilingual roles supported.

🔒

Your Data, Isolated

Each organisation runs on its own isolated cloud environment. Role-based access, audit trail and DPDP-aligned data handling.
