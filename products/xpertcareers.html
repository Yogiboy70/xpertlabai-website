The Problem

Most CV checkers fix your grammar. Recruiters reject you for something else.

Job seekers get the same advice everywhere: a template, a spell-check and a keyword score. But recruiters do not reject CVs for commas. They reject them because the story is unclear, the impact is missing or the role does not match the ambition.

xpertcareers.com is built on 25 years of reading CVs from the hiring side of the table — and benchmarks every CV against more than 10,000 CVs read across that career.

Free for everything the others charge for. AI where it actually matters.
