Illustrative scenario

How it works: month-end for a promoter-led distributor

Picture a distributor with 12 branches. Every month, branch accountants email Excel sheets to head office, where two people spend a week merging them, fixing formats and building the promoter's MIS.

  1. Map — we sit with the team for a few days and document exactly how the work is done today.
  2. Build — an AI agent collects the branch files, cleans and checks them, flags the exceptions and assembles the MIS.
  3. Run — the team reviews exceptions instead of building the report. The promoter gets the pack on day two, not day ten.

An illustration of how we approach automation, not a client result.
