AI Interviews & Assessments

A live AI interview.
Evidence in their own words.

Xpert Lens is a live, spoken interview with a lifelike AI interviewer, followed by a short proctored assessment. It produces a rich, visual report where every finding is backed by what the candidate actually said. Western assessment science, built for India — in English, Hinglish and Indian languages.

Request a DemoSee the pilot results
The Problem

First-round interviews eat your best people's time — and still run on gut feel.

Hiring managers and recruiters spend hours on first-round calls that mostly confirm what a good structured interview could have told them in twenty minutes. Notes are inconsistent. Two interviewers hear the same answer and score it differently. And personality questionnaires built on Western norms were never designed for Indian candidates.

Xpert Lens runs the same structured, spoken interview for every candidate, at any hour, in the language they are most comfortable in — and shows you exactly where each conclusion came from.

Fewer wasted interviews. Better-informed final rounds. A fairer process for every candidate.

How It Works

Talk. Answer. Understand.

1

Talk

The candidate has a real-time spoken conversation with a lifelike AI interviewer — in English, Hinglish or an Indian language. Follow-up questions adapt to their answers.

2

Answer

A short, timed, proctored assessment covers reasoning, critical thinking and situational judgement — with tab-switch and tempo signals feeding a trust score.

3

Understand

A visual report scores communication and competencies in honest bands, quotes the candidate's own words as evidence, and flags what to probe in the final round.
