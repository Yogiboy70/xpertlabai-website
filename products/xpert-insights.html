The Problem

Years of research. Sitting in files nobody has time to open.

Brands and research agencies spend heavily on brand tracking, usage-and-attitude studies and consumer interviews. The findings arrive as decks and data files. When a manager has a new question, an analyst spends days back in the spreadsheet and the statistics package — and the answer arrives after the decision has been made.

Generic AI tools are no answer: ask them about a survey and they will happily invent a number. In research, a figure that is one point off is a failed project.

Xpert Insights gives you the speed of a conversation with the rigour of your statistics team.

How It Stays Trustworthy

Numbers are computed.
Words are generated.
Every word points back to a number.

1

The Calculator

Every statistic is computed by ordinary, verifiable mathematics — exactly the way your analysts and your existing statistics software compute it. Not AI.

2

The Detective

When a number is weak, it digs: where in the funnel are consumers lost, which segments, which attributes are associated with it.

3

The Explainer

AI writes the finding in plain language — but only describes figures the calculator produced. A sentence with a number it can't trace is rejected.

Your research rules are applied without exception: small bases are suppressed, only statistically supported differences are called differences, and association is never described as cause.
