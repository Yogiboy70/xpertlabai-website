CRM & Sales Pipeline

Close more. Track everything.
Own your data.

Xpert Closr is a B2B CRM with sales discipline built in — live email, an AI deal assistant, approval workflows, recurring-revenue forecasting and mandatory win/loss capture. And it deploys on your own cloud, so your pipeline never sits on someone else's servers.

Request a DemoDeploy on Your Cloud
The Problem

Your pipeline lives in an Excel, three inboxes and the MD's head.

B2B sales in Indian MSMEs is relationship-led and approval-driven. Deals are tracked in a spreadsheet nobody fully trusts. Follow-ups depend on memory. Deals are lost without anyone recording why. And the global CRMs that promise to fix it are built for a different market, priced per seat, and hold your client data on their servers.

Xpert Closr is built for the way Indian B2B teams actually sell — and it runs inside your own cloud.

A CRM your team actually uses, because it does the work for them.

How It Works

From first contact
to signed deal.

1

Capture

Companies, contacts and deals in one place. Contacts are auto-tagged by function, so HR, finance and leadership outreach is one click.

2

Work the deal

Email is live inside the CRM. The AI assistant summarises every deal's history and drafts the next follow-up for you to approve and send.

3

Close & learn

Approvals for pricing and scope, a weighted recurring-revenue forecast, and a reason captured for every win and every loss.

What It Does

Seven features. One pipeline
that runs itself.

📬

Live Email Inside the CRM

Send, receive and thread email from the deal itself. Every conversation becomes part of the deal record.

🤖

AI Deal Assistant

Ask about any deal, get a summary of where it stands, and get a draft follow-up — you approve every send.

📊

Weighted, Recurring-Revenue Forecast

One-time and monthly fees captured per deal, so the forecast shows real annual value, not wishful totals.

✅

Approval Workflows

Pricing, discounts and scope changes go through a structured approve / reject with reason.

🏆

Mandatory Win/Loss Capture

Every closed deal requires a reason. Over time it becomes your most valuable sales intelligence.

👥

Bulk Personalised Outreach

Select contacts by function and send individual, personalised emails — no BCC blasts — with every touch logged.

🗓️

Activity & Task Discipline

Calls, meetings, tasks and follow-ups linked to accounts. Stale accounts and overdue tasks flagged automatically.

📈

Dashboards Leadership Opens

Pipeline by stage, win rates, revenue won and team activity — live, not compiled on Friday night.
