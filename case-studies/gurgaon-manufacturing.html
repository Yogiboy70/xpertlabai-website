Finally, one place to see them all.

The challenge

Hiring across plants and functions with no central thread.

For a pan-India manufacturing operation of this scale, recruitment never stops. Plants in multiple locations hire simultaneously — floor supervisors, engineers, quality managers, logistics heads — each site running its own process, its own spreadsheets, its own version of what was open and what had already been filled.

The central TA team had no single view of what was happening across locations. Roles were worked in parallel without coordination. Candidates were sometimes approached twice. Plant managers didn't know where things stood without calling someone. It wasn't a people problem — it was a visibility problem no spreadsheet network was ever going to fix.

The solution

Xpert ATS: one AI-ranked pipeline across every plant, every role.

Xpert ATS brought every open role — across all locations and functions — into a single consolidated pipeline. Every CV read and scored against the role by AI. Every stage tracked. The TA team and on-site hiring managers working from exactly the same picture for the first time.

Hiring managers review shortlists and record their verdict from a secure link. Shreyas, the built-in AI agent, answers questions about any candidate or requisition in plain English. And Hiring Memory keeps every offer, runner-up and shortlist ready for the next similar role.

Early deployment — the results here represent the first phase; the team continues to expand its use of the platform.
The results

50% more effective. From the first phase.

Within the first phase of deployment, the TA team measured a 50% improvement in overall effectiveness — roles progressing faster across locations, fewer candidates lost between plants, and a clear audit trail for every hiring decision at every site.

The single consolidated view didn't just save time. It changed how the team worked — from reactive and fragmented to coordinated and proactive. That shift is the foundation everything else is built on.

Hiring chaos is fixable.
It starts with visibility.

If your TA team is working across spreadsheets and group chats, Xpert ATS can change that in weeks — not months.
