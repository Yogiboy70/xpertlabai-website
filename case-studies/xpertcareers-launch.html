The insight

Job seekers get grammar fixes. Recruiters reject for something else.

Every CV tool on the market checks spelling, suggests a template and scores keywords. But after 25 years on the hiring side, we knew recruiters rarely reject a CV for a missing comma. They reject it because the story is unclear, the impact is invisible, or the CV doesn't match the role the person wants.

We also knew how Indian job seekers actually behave: they don't want to type essays, and many think and speak in Hinglish.

What we built

Examination. Diagnosis. Rebuild.

  • A free examination that reads the CV the way a recruiter would and marks career-level findings right on the page — with a clear score and a percentile against more than 10,000 CVs.
  • Free templates and PDF / Word downloads, matching what other platforms charge for.
  • An AI coach the candidate can talk to — in English or Hinglish — that builds the picture of what they actually do, with ticks and yes / no answers instead of typing.
  • A rebuilt CV tailored to the role they want.
Why it matters for us

The same intelligence, from both sides of the table.

xpertcareers.com is the candidate side of everything we build for employers. The same understanding of roles, skills and evidence that powers Xpert ATS and Xpert Lens helps a job seeker tell their story clearly — and over time, helps employers and candidates find each other.
